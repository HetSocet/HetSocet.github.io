- Logo reference: `public/logos/petfinderlogo.webp`

Final prompt:

> Use case: ads-marketing. Create one premium landscape 3:2 portfolio project cover image for the app "Pet Finder". Charming minimal designer collectible figurines, soft shadows. Rich tactile ceramic materials. Overall art-directed refined editorial 3D product render, tactile materials, soft large studio lighting, sophisticated spacing, off-white/charcoal portfolio harmony while respecting this app's brand colors.
